# Open Science Audit — Anesthesia & Critical Care (2015–2026)

## Project purpose
Cross-sectional meta-research audit of data, code, and model sharing in
anesthesia and critical care research published 2015–2026 in 10 leading
journals, with a nested arm evaluating clinical prediction model and AI
studies against TRIPOD+AI.
